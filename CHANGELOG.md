# Changelog

All notable user-facing changes, newest first. Categories:

**Added** (new features),
**Fixed** (bug and behaviour fixes),
**Removed** (things taken out),
**Info** (notes worth knowing).

## 2026-11-11

### Added
- **Audio mode choice**: Pick between the mod's volume leveling and the client's own.
- **Packet loss**: The connection readout shows packet loss for download and upload.
- **Idle word**: A new switch shows or hides the word "idle" in Server awareness.

### Fixed
- **Connection readout**: It now fits the bottom bar and no longer freezes.
- **Chat tabs**: The window can be dragged by the empty title bar next to the tabs again.
- **Talking indicator**: It lights up more reliably for people in other channels.
- **Image compression**: The size limit now tops out at 5 MB.

## 2026-11-10

### Added
- **First release**: Optional mods for the TeamSpeak 6 client on Linux, found under
  Settings > Mods. Everything is off by default, and nothing in TeamSpeak is changed.
- **Audio**: Evens out loud and quiet voices, for everyone or for each speaker.
- **Dashboard button**: A direct button for the Dashboard.
- **Connection quality**: Ping and packet loss at a glance.
- **Power tools**: Copy IDs from menus and a Ctrl+K command palette.
- **Server awareness**: See who is talking in other channels, and who joins or leaves.
- **Layout**: Chat tabs in the title bar and options to tidy up the interface.
- **Theme tools**: Pick and reload themes easily.
- **Split long messages**: Long chat messages are sent in several parts.
- **Compress images**: Large images are shrunk before upload.
- **Flatpak support**: Works with the Flatpak version of TeamSpeak too.
